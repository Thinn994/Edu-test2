### Status Reports:

#### Lê Quí Thịnh + Phan Đan Thần:

**Completed Tasks:**
- Researched platforms such as Google Classroom, Chamilo and Moodle
- Identified key features:
  - File upload
  - Quiz system
  - Class management
- Determined potential AI functionalities for the system, particularly in supporting automatic quiz generation and learning assistance.

**Issues/Obstacles:**
- Although AI features have been identified, their specifications remain insufficiently detailed.
- The exact behavior of the AI-based quiz generation is not clearly defined, including:
  - Whether the AI can generate multiple types of questions (e.g., multiple-choice, essay, or both)
  - For multiple-choice questions, whether they are single-answer or multiple-answer
  - How correct answers are determined and validated
  - Whether distractors (incorrect but plausible answers) are generated effectively
- Further clarification and detailed design of AI functionalities are required before implementation.

---

#### Phạm Đức Thịnh + Đỗ Khắc Thành Công

**Completed Tasks:**
- Identified target users:
  - Teachers and young private tutors teaching from high school level and above
  - Students attending extra classes or tutoring sessions

- Analyzed key benefits of the system:
  - For teachers/tutors:
    - Reduce the need for repeated printing of learning materials
    - Easily manage and organize lecture documents digitally
    - Avoid inconvenience such as carrying bulky paper materials or mixing up documents between different classes
  - For students:
    - Access full lecture materials at home if they are unable to take complete notes during in-person classes
    - Review lessons more effectively through stored digital content
    - Maintain flexibility to print materials themselves if they prefer traditional learning methods

**Issues/Obstacles:**
- Some students may not have personal devices (e.g., smartphones, laptops) or stable internet access to use the system effectively
